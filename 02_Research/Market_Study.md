# Market Study

## 1. Target Audience
The primary customers for the EcoBin system are:
- **Municipal Governments & City Councils:** Looking to reduce their carbon footprint and optimize sanitation budgets.
- **Private Waste Management Firms:** Looking to increase profit margins by reducing fleet fuel consumption and labor costs.
- **Large Campuses (Universities, Theme Parks):** Managing localized but dense waste ecosystems.

## 2. Market Size
The global smart waste management market size was valued at USD 1.83 billion in 2021 and is expected to expand at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 15.1% from 2022 to 2030. The push for "Smart Cities" is the primary driver.

## 3. Value Proposition
For an initial retrofit cost of ~$20 per bin (ESP32 + Ultrasonic), municipalities can expect a ~30% reduction in fleet operational costs (fuel and maintenance). The ROI timeline is estimated to be under 14 months for a medium-sized city.
